LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT (REVISED DRAFT)

This Agreement is made at Bengaluru on the 1st day of August 2026 between Mr. S. Raghavan, residing at No. 44, 3rd Cross, Indiranagar (hereinafter "the Licensor") and the undersigned occupant (hereinafter "the Licensee").

1. TERM: The licence is granted for a period of eleven (11) months commencing 1st August 2026, renewable by mutual written agreement of both parties upon terms to be agreed between them.

2. LICENCE FEE: The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 41,500 (Rupees Forty One Thousand Five Hundred only) on or before the 5th day of each calendar month, without demand or reminder. Any payment received after the 5th shall attract interest at 1.5% per month, simple, on the outstanding amount.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable deposit of Rs. 2,49,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Forty Nine Thousand only), equivalent to six months' licence fee. The said deposit shall be refunded within thirty (30) days of vacation of the premises, after deduction of amounts for damage beyond normal wear and tear, supported by receipts furnished to the Licensee.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD: The Licensee shall not vacate the premises before expiry of six (6) months. In the event the Licensee vacates prior thereto, the Licensee shall pay the licence fee for the remaining portion of the lock-in period only, and the Security Deposit shall not be forfeited.

5. ESCALATION: Upon any renewal, the licence fee shall stand increased by not more than 7% over the fee then prevailing.

6. ENTRY AND INSPECTION: The Licensor, his agents or representatives may enter the premises for the purposes of inspection only upon twenty-four (24) hours' prior written notice to the Licensee, and at a time convenient to the Licensee, save in the case of an emergency.

7. MAINTENANCE: Structural repairs, and repairs to plumbing, electrical wiring, seepage and waterproofing, shall be carried out by the Licensor at the Licensor's own cost. The Licensee shall bear the cost of day-to-day minor repairs arising from ordinary use.

8. ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CHARGE: In addition to the licence fee, the Licensee shall pay a non-refundable annual maintenance charge of Rs. 24,000 (Rupees Twenty Four Thousand only), payable in advance at the commencement of each year of the licence.

9. TERMINATION: Either party may terminate this Agreement after expiry of the lock-in period by giving sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other party.

10. ALTERATIONS: The Licensee shall make no permanent structural alteration to the premises without the prior written consent of the Licensor. The Licensee may install air conditioning units, water purifiers and curtain rods, and may remove the same on vacation provided any damage caused by removal is made good.

11. GUESTS AND OCCUPANCY: The premises shall be occupied solely by the Licensee and immediate family. Guests may stay overnight without restriction, provided the premises are not used as a commercial guest house.

12. INDEMNITY: The Licensee shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Licensor against all claims, damages, costs and expenses of whatsoever nature arising out of the Licensee's occupation of the premises, including claims by third parties, whether or not attributable to any act or omission of the Licensee.

13. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION: All costs of stamp duty, registration charges and professional fees in respect of this Agreement shall be shared equally between the Licensor and the Licensee.

14. DISPUTES: Any dispute arising hereunder shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual consent of the parties, whose decision shall be final and binding. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands on the date first written above.
