MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Meridian Outsourcing LLP ("Service Provider") and the undersigned client ("Client").

1. SCOPE: The Service Provider shall provide back-office processing services as described in Schedule A, which Schedule the Service Provider may amend from time to time by written notice to the Client.

2. FEES: The Client shall pay the monthly fee set out in Schedule A within seven (7) days of invoice. The Service Provider may revise fees upon thirty (30) days' notice, and continued use of the services after such notice shall constitute acceptance of the revised fees.

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL: This Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of thirty-six (36) months unless the Client gives written notice of non-renewal not less than one hundred and eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY: The aggregate liability of the Service Provider under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not in any circumstances exceed the fees paid by the Client in the one (1) month immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The Service Provider shall have no liability for loss of profit, loss of data, or business interruption.

5. INDEMNITY: The Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Service Provider, its officers, employees and subcontractors against any and all claims, losses, liabilities and expenses arising from or connected with the services, including claims arising from the Service Provider's own negligence, save only in the case of wilful misconduct proven by a court of final instance.

6. DATA: The Service Provider may retain, process and transfer Client data to any jurisdiction in which it or its subcontractors operate, and may use anonymised Client data for any purpose, including the development of its own products, in perpetuity.

7. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE: The Service Provider may terminate this Agreement at any time on fifteen (15) days' notice. The Client may terminate only for material breach remaining uncured after ninety (90) days' written notice, and shall in such event remain liable for the fees for the remainder of the then-current term.

8. SET-OFF: The Service Provider may set off any amount owed to the Client against any amount the Service Provider asserts is owed to it, without prior notice to the Client.
