OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

Vantage Systems Private Limited, having its registered office at Sector 62, Noida, is pleased to offer you the position of Software Engineer II on the following terms.

1. COMMENCEMENT AND PROBATION: Your employment shall commence on 1st October 2026. You shall be on probation for a period of twelve (12) months, which the Company may extend at its discretion without assigning any reason. During probation your services may be terminated with one (1) day's notice or without notice.

2. COMPENSATION: Your annual cost to company shall be Rs. 14,40,000, of which Rs. 4,00,000 is designated as a performance-linked variable component. The variable component shall be payable at the sole discretion of the Company, and no part of it shall be deemed earned until actually disbursed.

3. RETENTION BOND: In consideration of the training to be imparted to you, you agree to remain in continuous service of the Company for a minimum period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of joining. Should you resign or be terminated for cause before completion of the said period, you shall pay to the Company liquidated damages of Rs. 3,00,000, which sum the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss and not a penalty.

4. NOTICE PERIOD: You shall give the Company ninety (90) (ninety) days' written notice of resignation, or salary in lieu thereof calculated on full cost to company. The Company may terminate your employment by giving thirty (30) days' notice or salary in lieu thereof calculated on basic salary only.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: All inventions, works, designs, software, processes and improvements conceived or reduced to practice by you during the term of your employment, whether or not during working hours, whether or not using Company resources, and whether or not related to the business of the Company, shall vest absolutely in the Company. You hereby assign all present and future rights therein to the Company.

6. NON-COMPETE: For a period of twenty-four (24) months following cessation of your employment for any reason, you shall not directly or indirectly engage in, be employed by, consult for, or hold any interest in any business which competes with the Company anywhere in the Republic of India.

7. NON-SOLICITATION: For a period of twenty-four (24) months following cessation of your employment, you shall not solicit, approach or accept business from any client, customer or prospective client of the Company, nor solicit any employee of the Company.

8. MOONLIGHTING: You shall not, during the term of your employment, undertake any other employment, consultancy, freelance engagement, or contribute to any open source project, whether for consideration or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the Company.

9. DOCUMENTS: You shall deposit your original educational certificates with the Human Resources department on the date of joining. The said documents shall be returned to you upon completion of the retention period specified in Clause 3.

10. WORKING HOURS: Your working hours shall be from 9:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday. You acknowledge that the nature of your role may require you to work beyond these hours, on weekends and on public holidays, and that no additional remuneration shall be payable in respect thereof.

11. TRANSFER: The Company reserves the right to transfer your services to any of its offices, subsidiaries, affiliates or associate concerns, at any location in India or abroad, at its sole discretion, and you shall be bound to comply.

12. AMENDMENT OF POLICIES: The Company may amend, modify or withdraw any of its policies, benefits or terms of employment at any time without prior notice, and such amendment shall be binding upon you from the date of publication on the Company intranet.

13. GOVERNING LAW: This offer and your employment shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Noida shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Please sign and return a copy of this letter within seven (7) days, failing which this offer shall stand withdrawn.

For Vantage Systems Private Limited
Head — Human Resources
