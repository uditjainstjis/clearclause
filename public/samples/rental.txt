LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made at Bengaluru on the 1st day of August 2026 between Mr. S. Raghavan, residing at No. 44, 3rd Cross, Indiranagar (hereinafter "the Licensor") and the undersigned occupant (hereinafter "the Licensee").

1. TERM: The licence is granted for a period of eleven (11) months commencing 1st August 2026, renewable solely at the discretion of the Licensor upon such terms as the Licensor may then determine.

2. LICENCE FEE: The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 38,000 (Rupees Thirty Eight Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each calendar month, without demand or reminder. Any payment received after the 5th shall attract interest at 3% per month, compounded monthly, on the outstanding amount.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable deposit of Rs. 3,80,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Eighty Thousand only), equivalent to ten months' licence fee. The said deposit shall be refunded within ninety (90) days of vacation of the premises, after deduction of such amounts as the Licensor in his sole discretion deems appropriate towards repairs, repainting, cleaning and dues.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD: The Licensee shall not vacate the premises before expiry of the full eleven (11) months. In the event the Licensee vacates prior thereto, the Licensee shall remain liable to pay the entire balance licence fee for the unexpired portion of the lock-in period, and the Security Deposit shall stand forfeited in its entirety, notwithstanding anything contained elsewhere herein.

5. ESCALATION: Upon any renewal, the licence fee shall stand increased by not less than 12% over the fee then prevailing, irrespective of prevailing market rates.

6. ENTRY AND INSPECTION: The Licensor, his agents or representatives shall be entitled to enter the premises at any reasonable time for the purposes of inspection, and the Licensee shall not unreasonably withhold access. The Licensor shall endeavour to give prior intimation where practicable.

7. PAINTING AND RESTORATION: Irrespective of the condition of the premises at the time of vacation, the Licensee shall bear the cost of complete repainting of the premises, estimated at Rs. 45,000, which amount shall be deducted from the Security Deposit. The Licensee waives any right to obtain competing estimates.

8. MAINTENANCE: All repairs of every nature, whether structural or otherwise, including repairs to plumbing, electrical wiring, seepage and waterproofing, shall be carried out by the Licensee at the Licensee's own cost. The Licensor shall bear no liability whatsoever in respect thereof.

9. TERMINATION BY LICENSOR: The Licensor may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving fifteen (15) days' written notice to the Licensee, without assigning any reason. The Licensee may terminate only after expiry of the lock-in period, and upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

10. ALTERATIONS: The Licensee shall make no alteration, addition or fixture to the premises, including the installation of air conditioning units, water purifiers or curtain rods, without the prior written consent of the Licensor. Any such installation shall on vacation become the absolute property of the Licensor without compensation.

11. GUESTS AND OCCUPANCY: The premises shall be occupied solely by the Licensee and immediate family. No guest shall stay overnight for more than three consecutive nights without the written permission of the Licensor.

12. INDEMNITY: The Licensee shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Licensor against all claims, damages, costs and expenses of whatsoever nature arising out of the Licensee's occupation of the premises, including claims by third parties, whether or not attributable to any act or omission of the Licensee.

13. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION: All costs of stamp duty, registration charges and professional fees in respect of this Agreement shall be borne entirely by the Licensee.

14. DISPUTES: Any dispute arising hereunder shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator to be appointed by the Licensor, whose decision shall be final and binding. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands on the date first written above.
